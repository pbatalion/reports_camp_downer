What This Number Tells You

Camp Downer's tiered pricing model covers its costs. At $648 average net tuition (after scholarships) vs. $586 true cost per camper week, tuition alone keeps the camp sustainable — no fundraising required to break even. The ~$62/week margin (about 10%) builds reserves and funds facility investment. Across the 1,113 camper weeks delivered this summer, that scales to the roughly $68K surplus projected for the year.

How "Cost Per Camper Week" Is Calculated

A camper week is one camper attending for one week. Camp Downer delivered 1,113 camper weeks in Summer 2026 — from 968 session registrations, counting the two-week Session 3 (145 campers) as two weeks each: 823 one-week registrations + (145 × 2) = 1,113.

True cost per camper week = total annual operating budget ÷ total camper weeks
= $652,440 ÷ 1,113 ≈ $586
This spreads every operating cost — staff, food, programs, facilities, admin, and insurance — evenly across all the weeks of camp delivered. It's the all-in cost to serve one camper for one week.

Average net tuition per camper week = net tuition revenue ÷ total camper weeks
= $720,825 ÷ 1,113 ≈ $648 — net tuition is the $748,185 enrolled less $27,360 in scholarships, blended across the three pricing tiers.

Because average net tuition ($648) sits above true cost ($586), tuition alone covers operations — the +$62/week gap (about 10%) builds reserves and funds facility investment. Camper-week count is from the final Summer 2026 registration summary.
