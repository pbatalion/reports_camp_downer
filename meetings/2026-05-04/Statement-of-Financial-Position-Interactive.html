About Fixed Assets: Camp Downer operates on 84 acres of state forest land under a lease with the Vermont Department of Forests, Parks and Recreation (through 2041). We don't own the land or buildings — the state does. The $567K in "Fixed Assets" represents the accounting book value of improvements we've made (dining hall, pool, cabins, etc.), not property ownership. These improvements become state property at lease termination. This is standard "leasehold improvement" accounting — we track the value for financial reporting, but it's paper value rather than something we could sell.
