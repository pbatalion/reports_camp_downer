What This Number Tells You

Camp Downer's tiered pricing model is working. At $1,695 average tuition vs. $1,553 true cost, the camp is financially sustainable without requiring fundraising to cover operations. The $142/week margin funds scholarships, builds reserves, and invests in facilities. This is the opposite of most camps, where cost exceeds tuition and the gap becomes the fundraising story.
