What This Means for Camp Downer

Current Position: Strong

$855K cash on hand as of April 27. This is peak cash season as registration payments flow in before summer expenses begin.

Summer Burn Rate

Expect ~$200K/month in expenses during June-August (staff salaries, food service, programs). Cash will decline rapidly.

The Danger Zone

Sept-Nov: Camp revenue stops but fixed costs continue (insurance, utilities, year-round staff). This is when most camps feel the pinch.

Recovery

December: Early bird registrations for next summer begin. Cash starts rebuilding for the next cycle.

Key Question for the Board

Do you know your lowest cash month? For most summer camps, it's October or November. Camp Downer's strong reserves ($855K starting) should weather this well, but it's worth tracking monthly to ensure the projection holds.

Camp Downer's Safety Net

Even at the projected low point (~$300K in November), Camp Downer would have:

  • 5+ months of operating reserves (at ~$54K/month average)
  • $531K in investments at Fidelity (accessible if truly needed)
  • No debt — only routine monthly payables

Bottom line: Cash timing is healthy. The organization can survive a bad summer.
