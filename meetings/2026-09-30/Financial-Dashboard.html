The season is closed. All seven sessions have run, tuition is collected, and summer costs are paid. Net revenue stands at $151,252 against a budget that planned for a $35,738 deficit. Expenses finished 12% under plan, and the registration line sits above budget as it does every year, because the budget is built on a single rate rather than the three pricing tiers. About $74,302 of budget remains unspent, but the Directors' Annual Report explains most of it: some was spent and capitalised to the balance sheet, and some is still to come.
How the ~$125K year-end figure is reached
Net revenue today is $151,252. The expense budget has $74,302 unspent. The Directors' Annual Report accounts for it line by line, and most of it is not money that will simply be saved:
Off-season projects and equipment (spent, but capitalised to the balance sheet) $16,579 already spent
Dining service (under only because of $14,289 of 2024–25 credits) $13,824 not a saving
Staff salaries (year-round director role; expected to finish only ~$5,000 under) ~$17,836 still to come
Admin, utilities, payroll fees, director line (expected to meet or exceed budget) ~$8,006 still to come
Total realistically still to spend ~$25,842
$151,252 less about $25,842 gives a year-end result near $125,000, and closer to $113,000 if year-end accounting and insurance also land in full. Any Summer 2027 deposits received before December 31 would add to it, as would a 2026 dining credit if Fresh Picks issues one.
The books are now current. The Directors' Annual Report had flagged a QuickBooks receipt backlog from the summer. The accountant has since confirmed the books are up to date, and these figures are the reconciled September 25 exports. Clearing the backlog added $3,598 of expenses, two thirds of it one electric bill, and $500 of scholarship donations, taking net revenue from $154,350 to $151,252. The earlier caveat that expenses might be understated no longer applies.

For Discussion

The 2027 budget is set at the November meeting, so the items below are flagged here for context rather than for decision today. Detail is drawn from the Directors' Annual Report, whose own finance section recommends updating budgets based on this year's over and under spending.

  • Where the surplus sits (November decision). The surplus accumulates in the operating account by default, which now holds $367,614 in checking alongside $254,325 in the money market. Whether to move some of it into the Fidelity portfolio is worth deciding in November rather than letting it sit by inertia. The Directors' report carries a long list of capital candidates: a replacement camp vehicle, Rinnai water heater replacement, a new freezer door, kitchen equipment and cabin rewiring.
  • The off-season budget was spent, just not shown as an expense. The maintenance shed renovation and water system update were capitalised to Leasehold Improvements. The Directors' report lists further work awaiting estimates: rewiring cabins, hardwiring exhaust fans, steel gas lines and draining well #3, plus the Director's Cabin roof and the Rec Hall floor.
  • One overage is a one-off, the other is not. The truck's $3,431 was specific repairs, and the leaf-spring job is already scheduled for May 2027. Healthcare is different: the Directors' report says these costs "continue to increase" and asks for a higher budget in 2027, alongside a request to purchase an AED.
