What This Shows: The Statement of Activity is the nonprofit version of a profit-and-loss statement. It covers a period of time: Revenue minus Expenditures, plus net Other activity, equals Net Revenue. This is the first view of a completed season. All seven sessions have run, the revenue is collected, and the summer's costs have been paid.
The Headline: $151,252 in net revenue against a budget that planned for a deficit of $35,738. Part of that gap is structural: the budget is built on a single rate and does not model the three pricing tiers, so registration revenue sits above the budgeted line every year. The rest is expenses finishing 12% under plan.

Bottom Line

NET REVENUE (YEAR TO DATE) $151,252.19
What's still ahead: $74,302 of the annual expense budget is unspent, but the Directors' Annual Report accounts for it. Some was in fact spent and capitalised to the balance sheet (the maintenance shed renovation, the water system update, the new washer and dryer). Dining is under only because of $14,289 of credits carried in from 2024 and 2025. Roughly $25,842 is genuinely still to come, mostly the year-round director salary, which keeps accruing monthly after camp closes, plus administration, utilities and payroll costs. That would bring the year to about $125,000 against a budget that planned to lose $35,738. Fall deposits for Summer 2027 would push that higher.
